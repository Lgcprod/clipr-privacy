Clipr — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 August 2026

Clipr collects nothing. It has no account, no analytics, no advertising, and no networking code of any kind. Everything you save stays on your iPhone. Nothing is ever sent to us, because there is nowhere for it to be sent.

What Clipr stores, and where

The clips you save — text, links, phone numbers, email addresses, postal addresses — are stored in a private container on your device, shared only between the Clipr app and its own keyboard and Messages extensions. Your folders, notes, and settings live in the same place.

This data never leaves your device unless you deliberately export it (see Backups below).

Your clipboard

Clipr does not monitor your clipboard. It does not run in the background watching what you copy, and it does not check your clipboard when it opens.

Your clipboard is read at exactly one moment: when you tap a button asking Clipr to save what you copied. iOS may show its own paste confirmation when that happens — that prompt appears because you just asked for a paste, and it is the system telling you the truth about what is going on.

The Clipr keyboard

The optional Clipr keyboard requires Full Access from iOS. It needs this for one reason: to read the clips you have already saved out of Clipr's own shared storage so it can insert them.

The keyboard does not record what you type, does not log your keystrokes, and does not transmit anything anywhere. When you tap “+” to save the field you are editing, Clipr reads that field at that moment and shows it to you before anything is stored. iOS never exposes secure fields such as passwords to third-party keyboards, so those cannot be read at all.

The Messages extension

Clipr's panel in the Messages app shows the clips you have already saved and inserts one into your message when you tap it. It never sends a message on your behalf — you always press send yourself. It cannot read your conversations or the message you are composing.

Face ID

If you turn on the optional Face ID lock, authentication is handled entirely by iOS. Clipr is told only whether authentication succeeded. Your biometric data is never available to Clipr and is never stored by it.

Backups

Clipr can export your clips as a plain JSON file. This only happens when you choose to export, and the file is saved wherever you tell iOS to put it — a location you pick. Clipr does not upload backups anywhere.

Third parties

There are none. Clipr contains no third-party SDKs, no analytics frameworks, no crash reporting, and no advertising libraries. No data is shared with anyone, because none is collected.

Children

Clipr is safe for all ages. It collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If Clipr ever gains a feature that changes any of the above — optional iCloud sync, for example — this policy will be updated before that feature ships, and the change will be described in the app's release notes.
